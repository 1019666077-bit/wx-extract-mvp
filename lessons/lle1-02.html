对话 · 中英对照

Jonathan

Hey, Pete! Who’s your friend?

嘿，皮特！你的朋友是谁？

Pete

She is Anna. She is new to D.C.

她是安娜。她刚到华盛顿特区。

Jonathan

Where are you from?

你来自哪里？

Anna

I am from a small town.

我来自一个小镇。

Jonathan

Well, welcome to D.C.

好的，欢迎来到华盛顿特区。

Anna

Thank you.

谢谢。

Jonathan

I am Jonathan. I am in apartment B4.

我是乔纳森。我住在 B4 公寓。

Anna

I am in apartment C2. Marsha is my roommate.

我住在 C2 公寓。玛莎是我的室友。

Jonathan

I know Marsha. She is nice.

我认识玛莎。她人很好。

Pete

And I am in Apartment D7. I have to go now.

我住在 D7 公寓。我现在得走了。

Anna

Oh!

哦！

Pete

Remember to call Marsha at work. Tell her you’re here.

记得给上班的玛莎打电话。告诉她你到了。

Anna

Right, thanks, Pete. Nice to meet you!

好的，谢谢，皮特。很高兴认识你！

Jonathan

You too, Bye.

我也是，再见。

Anna

Apartment C2, here I come!

C2 公寓，我来了！

听力小测验

1. Where is Anna from?
  • A big city
  • A small town
  • New York City
2. Who lives in apartment B4?
  • Pete
  • Jonathan
  • Marsha
3. Who is Marsha?
  • Pete's roommate
  • Anna's roommate
  • Jonathan's roommate
