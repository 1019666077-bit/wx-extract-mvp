对话 · 中英对照

Anna

Pete, hi! Hi, we are here!

皮特，嗨！嗨，我们到了！

Pete

Hi, Anna! Hi, Marsha!

嗨，安娜！嗨，玛莎！

Anna

Hi!

嗨！

Pete

How are you two?

你们俩好吗？

Marsha

I am great!

我很好！

Anna

You know, Pete, I am new to D.C. The city is big.

你知道的，皮特，我刚到华盛顿特区。这座城市很大。

Pete

Yeah. But you learn a little more every day. How's the new apartment?

是啊。不过你每天都会多学一点。新公寓怎么样？

Anna

The new apartment is great! Let's get coffee!

新公寓很棒！我们去喝咖啡吧！

Marsha

Anna, do you have a pen?

安娜，你有笔吗？

Anna

Yes. I have a pen in my bag.

有。我包里有一支笔。

Anna

I have a …

我有一个……

Pete

It is not a pen. It is a book. It is a big book.

那不是笔。那是一本书。一本很大的书。

Anna

Yes. Yes it is, Pete. I know I have a pen, though…

对。是的，皮特。不过我知道我有一支笔……

Anna

I have a ....

我有一个……

Marsha

It is not a pen, Anna ... a toy?

那不是笔，安娜……是玩具？

Anna

I have a ....

我有一个……

Pete

And it is a pillow!

那是一个枕头！

Anna

Pete, Marsha, I know I have a pen.

皮特，玛莎，我知道我有一支笔。

Marsha

Anna, it is a map.

安娜，那是一张地图。

Pete

Why do you have a map of the world?

你为什么带着一张世界地图？

Anna

Pete, Marsha. Now I know I have a pen.

皮特，玛莎。现在我知道我有一支笔了。

Pete

And now you have a lamp.

现在你又拿出一盏灯。

Marsha

Anna.

安娜。

Anna

I have a pen! Let’s get coffee!

我有笔了！我们去喝咖啡吧！

听力小测验

1. What does Marsha ask Anna for?
  • A book
  • A pen
  • A map
2. What does Pete say about the first thing from Anna's bag?
  • It is a pen
  • It is a lamp
  • It is a book. It is a big book
3. What does Anna finally find?
  • A pen
  • A pillow
  • A toy
