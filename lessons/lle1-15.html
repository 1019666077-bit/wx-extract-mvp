Anna

Hello! People from all over the world come to Washington, D.C. When I’m at work, I love eating lunch outside. I like to watch people walking by. They all look very different. Today, my friend Ashley is eating lunch with me.

嗨！世界各地的人都会来华盛顿特区。上班的时候，我喜欢在外面吃午饭。我喜欢看路过的人。他们看起来都不一样。今天，我的朋友阿什莉和我一起吃午饭。

Anna

Ashley, today the weather is beautiful, isn’t it?

阿什莉，今天天气真好，对吧？
