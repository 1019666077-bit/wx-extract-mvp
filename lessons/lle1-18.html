Anna

Hello, from Washington, D.C.! Today at work I am reading the news for the first time. I am really nervous. But my boss, Ms. Weaver, is here to help me.

你好，这里是华盛顿特区！今天上班我第一次读新闻。我很紧张。不过我的老板韦弗女士在这儿帮我。

Caty

Now, Anna, remember. When we read the news we are always reading facts. We never show our feelings.

安娜，记住。我们读新闻的时候，读的永远是事实。我们从不表现自己的感情。
