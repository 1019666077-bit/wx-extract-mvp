VOA慢速英文第3課 I'm Here!

Level 1 · 第3課

這是 VOA Let's Learn English 慢速英文 Level 1 第 3 課「I'm Here!」。本課可以免費試學：播放聽力影片，看中英對照字幕，再做三道小測驗。

對話 · 中英對照

Anna

I am in my new apartment! Great!

我在我的新公寓裡了！太好了！

Anna

I live with Marsha. We’re roommates.

我和瑪莎住在一起。我們是室友。

Anna

I want to cook dinner. Oh! Hmmm. Is there a supermarket near here?

我想做晚飯。哦！嗯……這附近有超市嗎？

Anna

Marsha knows. Marsha's work number is 555-8986.

瑪莎知道。瑪莎上班的電話是 555-8986。

Man

Hello.

喂。

Anna

Hello. Is this Marsha? It is Anna.

你好。是瑪莎嗎？我是安娜。

Man

I am sorry. You have the wrong number.

對不起。你打錯電話了。

Anna

Oh. Is this 555-8986?

哦。這是 555-8986 嗎？

Man

No. This is 555-8689.

不是。這是 555-8689。

Anna

Oh. Excuse me!

哦。不好意思！

Man

Okay. 'Bye.

好的。再見。

Anna

One more time. 555-8986. Please be Marsha.

再打一次。555-8986。希望是瑪莎。

Marsha

Hello. This is Marsha.

喂。我是瑪莎。

Anna

Yes, Marsha. I want to cook dinner.

是的，瑪莎。我想做晚飯。

Marsha

Excuse me? Anna? Is that you?

什麼？安娜？是你嗎？

Anna

Oh, yes. I am here!

哦，是的。我到了！

Marsha

Good. You are there.

很好。你在那裡。

Anna

I want to find a supermarket.

我想找一家超市。

Marsha

Oh, okay. The supermarket is at 1500 Irving Street. It is near the apartment.

哦，好的。超市在歐文街 1500 號。就在公寓附近。

Anna

Great! Goodbye!

太好了！再見！

Marsha

Goodbye, Anna.

再見，安娜。

Anna

There is a big supermarket on our street. And Marsha says I am a good cook! Until next time! Bye!

我們這條街上有一家很大的超市。瑪莎還說我很會做飯！下次見！再見！

聽力小測驗

1. What does Anna say about Marsha?
  • Marsha is her best friend
  • She and Marsha are roommates
  • Marsha lives on another street
2. Why does the man say he is sorry?
  • Anna has the wrong number
  • Marsha is not at work
  • He does not know Anna
3. What does Marsha say about the supermarket?
  • It is on another street
  • It is at 1500 Main Street
  • It is at 1500 Irving Street, near the apartment
