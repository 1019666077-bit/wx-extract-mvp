VOA慢速英文第4課 What Is It?

Level 1 · 第4課

這是 VOA Let's Learn English 慢速英文 Level 1 第 4 課「What Is It?」。本課可以免費試學：播放聽力影片，看中英對照字幕，再做三道小測驗。

對話 · 中英對照

Anna

Pete, hi! Hi, we are here!

皮特，嗨！嗨，我們到了！

Pete

Hi, Anna! Hi, Marsha!

嗨，安娜！嗨，瑪莎！

Anna

Hi!

嗨！

Pete

How are you two?

你們倆好嗎？

Marsha

I am great!

我很好！

Anna

You know, Pete, I am new to D.C. The city is big.

你知道的，皮特，我剛到華盛頓特區。這座城市很大。

Pete

Yeah. But you learn a little more every day. How's the new apartment?

是啊。不過你每天都會多學一點。新公寓怎麼樣？

Anna

The new apartment is great! Let's get coffee!

新公寓很棒！我們去喝咖啡吧！

Marsha

Anna, do you have a pen?

安娜，你有筆嗎？

Anna

Yes. I have a pen in my bag.

有。我包裡有一支筆。

Anna

I have a …

我有一個……

Pete

It is not a pen. It is a book. It is a big book.

那不是筆。那是一本書。一本很大的書。

Anna

Yes. Yes it is, Pete. I know I have a pen, though…

對。是的，皮特。不過我知道我有一支筆……

Anna

I have a ....

我有一個……

Marsha

It is not a pen, Anna ... a toy?

那不是筆，安娜……是玩具？

Anna

I have a ....

我有一個……

Pete

And it is a pillow!

那是一個枕頭！

Anna

Pete, Marsha, I know I have a pen.

皮特，瑪莎，我知道我有一支筆。

Marsha

Anna, it is a map.

安娜，那是一張地圖。

Pete

Why do you have a map of the world?

你為什麼帶著一張世界地圖？

Anna

Pete, Marsha. Now I know I have a pen.

皮特，瑪莎。現在我知道我有一支筆了。

Pete

And now you have a lamp.

現在你又拿出一盞燈。

Marsha

Anna.

安娜。

Anna

I have a pen! Let’s get coffee!

我有筆了！我們去喝咖啡吧！

聽力小測驗

1. What does Marsha ask Anna for?
  • A book
  • A pen
  • A map
2. What does Pete say about the first thing from Anna's bag?
  • It is a pen
  • It is a lamp
  • It is a book. It is a big book
3. What does Anna finally find?
  • A pen
  • A pillow
  • A toy
