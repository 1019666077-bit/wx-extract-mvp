對話 · 中英對照

Anna

Hello, everyone! Today my friend Marsha is at her friend's house.

大家好！今天我的朋友瑪莎在她朋友家裡。

Anna

She says it is beautiful. I want to see this house! Here we are!

她說那房子很漂亮。我想去看看！我們到了！

Anna

Marsha, I am in the kitchen! It is a beautiful kitchen!

瑪莎，我在廚房裡！這廚房很漂亮！

Marsha

It is beautiful. We cook in the kitchen.

是很漂亮。我們在廚房做飯。

Anna

I eat in the kitchen.

我在廚房吃飯。

Marsha

We relax in the living room.

我們在客廳休息。

Anna

I relax in the living room. Marsha, let’s go upstairs!

我在客廳休息。瑪莎，我們上樓吧！

Marsha

Anna? Where are you?

安娜？你在哪裡？

Anna

Marsha, I am in the bathroom! I wash in the bathroom.

瑪莎，我在浴室裡！我在浴室洗澡。

Marsha

I am in the bedroom. We sleep in the bedroom.

我在臥室裡。我們在臥室睡覺。

Anna

I sleep in the bedroom!

我在臥室睡覺！

聽力小測驗

1. Where is Marsha today?
  • At her office
  • At her friend's house
  • At the supermarket
2. Where do they cook?
  • In the living room
  • In the bedroom
  • In the kitchen
3. Where is Anna when Marsha asks, "Where are you?"
  • In the bathroom
  • In the kitchen
  • In the living room
